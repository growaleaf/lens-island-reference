Sources & caveats:
  • Weapons, tools, torches & watering cans — , by Robert N Adams, published 2021-11-29. Re-fetched 2026-11-13 via the Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2025-05-24 (archived copy) because a direct curl returned HTTP 403. The live page may have been edited since that snapshot — the article is presented as a living guide.
  • No calculator, planner, or database exists for Len's Island at time of writing. A second non-Fandom source with comparable stat tables could not be found (one promising search result, a MagicGameWorld beginner's guide, 404s).
  • lens-island.fandom.com was not crawled — off limits per the build charter.
  • Backpacks are tracked by the source but are inventory-capacity items, not weapons or tools, so they are out of scope for this reference and are not included.
  • Blanks are shown as "not published," never invented. Where a stat label itself looks inconsistent in the source (for example one shield's third stat column being labeled "Critical Hits" instead of "Speed Modifier" like its peers), the label is reproduced exactly as published rather than silently corrected.
